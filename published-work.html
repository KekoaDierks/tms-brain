UNIVERSITY OF PORTLAND RESEARCH

Previous University of Portland research work

The current project builds on a broader line of neural engineering and TMS research involving University of Portland researchers. The conference works below cover neuronal strength-duration behavior, ultra-high frequency TMS, coil orientation, and coil and pulse optimization.

  1. 2026

    Carter Rhoades, Peter Asbeck, Vincent Leung, Milan Makale, and Shaghayegh Abbasi, “Neuronal Strength-Duration Characteristics as Design Parameters for Closed-Loop Neural Interfaces”, 22nd Annual International IEEE EMBS Conference on Body Sensor Networks, Porto, Portugal, October 10 – 12, 2026.

  2. 2025

    Shaghayegh Abbasi, Carter Rhoades, Vincent Leung, Peter Asbeck, and Milan Makale, “Ultra-High Frequency Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) for Treatment of Depression”, 12th International IEEE EMBS Conference on Neural Engineering, San Diego, California, November 11 – 14, 2025.

  3. 2024

    Shaghayegh Abbasi, Matt David, Vincent Leung, Peter Asbeck, and Milan Makale, “Coil Orientation and Stimulation Threshold in Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS)”, 46th Annual International Conference of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society, Orlando, Florida, July 15 – 19, 2024.

  4. 2023

    Shaghayegh Abbasi, Benjamin Joray, Kenneth Rudnicki, Vincent Leung, Peter Asbeck, and Milan Makale, “Coil Size and Current Pulse Optimization through Multi-Scale Modeling for Repetitive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (rTMS)”, 11th International IEEE EMBS Conference on Neural Engineering, Baltimore, Maryland, April 25 – 27, 2023.
