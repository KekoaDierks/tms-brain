About the Project

This website presents one part of a larger collaborative research effort studying transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS). The work shown here is carried out from the University of Portland as part of that broader team.

TRANSCRANIAL MAGNETIC STIMULATION

What TMS is used for

TMS is a noninvasive form of brain stimulation that uses rapidly changing magnetic fields to induce electric fields in the brain. It is used clinically for conditions including depression, and this site focuses on understanding TMS for depression through computational modeling.

Part of a larger team: This site focuses on research being conducted from the University of Portland, including work on brain regions, neuron morphology, and stimulation parameters. Partner teams contribute other parts of the broader research effort.
